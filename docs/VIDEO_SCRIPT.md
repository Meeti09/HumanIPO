# Product video script — 45 seconds

**Rules:** real UI only, no mockups, no emojis, no stock footage. Screen recording at 1920×1080,
browser zoom 100%, transaction panel visible in the bottom-right the whole time. Record the whole
flow in one take against the live deployment so the confirmation timings are genuine.

Voiceover is optional — the captions carry it. If you do record VO, keep it flat and unhurried.

---

| Time | On screen | Caption / VO |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:05 | Landing page, slow scroll to the worked-example card | **"A fixed loan wants the same payment whether you landed the job or not."** |
| 0:05–0:10 | Worked-example card fills the frame: 5,000 raise · 7% · 36 months · 12,000 cap | **"An income share agreement asks for a percentage of what you actually earn."** |
| 0:10–0:20 | Explore → open Sarah Mehta. Type 500 in the invest panel. Ownership preview updates. Click Invest. Approve + fund confirm. | **"Fund someone. Your contribution is a proportional claim on every future settlement."** |
| 0:20–0:26 | Create page → publish. New agreement contract page loads. | **"Publishing deploys your own agreement contract."** |
| 0:26–0:34 | Recipient panel. Type 4000 in the income field. The "You will owe" row updates to 280. Click Submit income. | **"Report income. The contract applies the percentage, the floor, and the remaining cap — 4,000 becomes 280."** |
| 0:34–0:40 | Click Settle. Transaction panel shows the confirmation time. Cut to Portfolio: claimable amount appears. Click Claim. | **"Settle, and it splits across funders automatically. Every step is a real transaction on Monad."** |
| 0:40–0:45 | Cut to Monad Explorer showing the agreement's transaction list. Then a white frame with the wordmark. | **"HumanYield — fund a person's future."** then **https://humanipo.vercel.app** |

---

## Must appear on screen

- At least one transaction confirmation time (this is the Monad point — do not cut it)
- The income → obligation calculation updating live (this is the ISA point)
- The Monad Explorer tab with real transactions
- The testnet/demo-asset disclaimer, at least once, legibly

## Must not appear

- Any figure presented as a real-world return
- Fake user counts, TVL, testimonials or partner logos
- A mocked-up screen that does not exist in the product
